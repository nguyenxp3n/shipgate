# Product Specification Template

## Purpose and users
Describe the problem, intended users/actors, value, release scope, non-goals, and success criteria in product/domain terms.

## Capabilities and journeys
For each user-facing capability state preconditions, happy path, alternate paths, authorization expectations, user-visible failures, and observable completion. Distinguish release-required from explicitly disabled/future capability.

## Business rules and invariants
Assign stable IDs to load-bearing business rules and state transitions. Identify authoritative state, allowed transitions, forbidden transitions, and externally visible side effects.

## Protected unknowns
Record unresolved product behavior as Decision Requests. Do not select infrastructure or implementation shortcuts to resolve product ambiguity.
